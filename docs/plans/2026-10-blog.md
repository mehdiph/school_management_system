Two findings changed the implementation:
- **Paragraphs.** Trix 2.1 has no real paragraphs: Enter adds `<br>` inside one block, even
  with the default block configured as `<p>` (which also put figures inside `<p>`).
  Trix's default `<div>` blocks are kept, and the server makes paragraphs: a blank line
  (two `<br>`) starts a new `<p>`, a single Enter stays a line break. That matches what
  the author sees in the editor.
- **Configuration order.** The UMD build defines its elements as it loads, so a script
  after it is too late to change the toolbar. The toolbar is rendered by the template (in
  Persian), and `editor.js`, loaded **before** Trix, configures it on
  `trix-before-initialize`.

Verdict: Trix passes; no fallback to Quill.

### 1.6 Decisions and ambiguities resolved in Phase 1

1. **Paragraphs from blank lines** (§1.2): one Enter is a line break, a blank line a new
   paragraph.
2. **Caption = alt for inline images.** Trix has no alt field, only a caption. The caption
   is written as both `alt` and `<figcaption>`; the editor's caption placeholder says so,
   and the checklist's line about alt texts covers it.
3. **`Tag.key`** (not in the plan's model): the name folded for spelling, unique. The plan
   asked for "normalised, unique" names; folding ZWNJ and spaces needs a separate column,
   because the displayed name keeps its ZWNJ.
4. **`Post.search_text`** (not in the plan's model): the folded key of title, excerpt and
   text, so «کتاب‌خوانی»، «کتاب خوانی» and «کتابخوانی» find each other (finding 14).
   `body_text` keeps readable text for the excerpt and reading time.
5. **Display normalisation is conservative.** ي/ى→ی, ك→ک, tatweel and spacing are fixed;
   ئ, ۀ and the hamza forms are folded only for search («مسئله» must stay «مسئله»).
6. **Publishing a draft.** The plan's diagram publishes from submitted or archived. Until
   Phase 3, editors write posts in the admin, and those are drafts. A draft can therefore be
   published from its own review page, which warns that the author never sent it; the bulk
   action still publishes only submitted and archived posts.
7. **Return to the author** works from submitted, published and archived (D9: "an editor
   can return it to draft"); it needs a note and unfeatures the post. Submitting clears an
   old note.
8. **`BlogAuthor.user` is `PROTECT`** and its choices are eligible roles **plus existing
   authors**: otherwise an author whose role changed could not even be opened to switch them
   off.
9. **Category required** when the section has active categories (D13), in `Post.clean`.
10. **Images in the admin come after the first save:** an upload needs a post to belong to.
    The editor says so instead of offering the button.
11. **Orphan inline images** are pruned on save only when a day old, so an image uploaded
    into an editor that hasn't saved yet is never deleted under it.
12. **Inline images and covers become JPEG** at most 1600 px through the existing
    `website.images.reencode`, like the site's portraits.
13. **One editor script for both places:** `blog/js/editor.js` and `css/editor.css` (the
    plan said `write.js` / `write.css`) serve the admin now and the authoring area in
    Phase 3, which adds its own small script for the rest.
14. **`unpublish` also unfeatures** the post, so a featured slot never points at an
    archived post.
15. **The admin's upload endpoint** (`admin/blog/post/<id>/upload-image/`) serves editors in
    Phase 1; the authoring area gets its own in Phase 3, through the same
    `services.add_image`.

## Phase 2: the public blog

### 2.1 Summary

The blog is public at `/blog/`: home, section, category, tag, author and search pages, the
post page, the preview for authors and editors, Atom feeds, the sitemap and `robots.txt`.
Every page works without JS, sends a strict CSP, costs a fixed number of queries and has no
horizontal scroll at 375 px. The design is «روزنامه دیواری», the school's wall newspaper:
the blog's own look inside the public site's navbar and footer (the blog is a new public
area, so it gets its own direction; the navbar, footer and fonts are the site's). Around it,
the navbar's «وبلاگ» row now points to `/blog/` (website `0014`), the navbar marks the
section you're in, and a teacher card links to its author's blog page.

The public rule is one `Q` (`blog.models.post.public_q`), used by `Post.objects.public()`,
the admin's lists, the sitemap and the counts. Search goes through `search_text` (any
spelling, two letters at least).

### 2.4 URLs and who sees what

The URL table is in `docs/apps/blog.md` §6. Every public page is GET/HEAD only
(`require_safe`; anything else is a 405).

| Who | Public posts and pages | Drafts, submitted, scheduled, archived; inactive sections | `/blog/preview/<id>/` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anyone, logged in or not | ✓ | 404 | 404 |
| The post's author, while they can write (ADR-026) | ✓ | 404 at the public URL | ✓ (`noindex`, a banner, only the post) |
| Editor (`blog.publish_post`), superuser | ✓ | 404 at the public URL | ✓ |
| Another author, a staff member without the permission | ✓ | 404 | 404 |

A preview of a post that is public redirects to its public URL. The preview never shows
related posts or neighbours, so it can't leak another unpublished post.

### 2.5 Decisions and ambiguities resolved in Phase 2

1. **Previous/next stay in the section.** A post's neighbours are the next newer and older
   public posts of the same section, so «مشاوره» reading doesn't jump into «پرورشی».
2. **Related posts:** same category first, then shared tags, then the same section; newest
   first in each; three at most. Without the section fallback most posts would show none
   while the blog is small.
3. **Featured posts are left out of «تازه‌ها»** on every page of the home, so paging doesn't
   repeat them; the featured row, sections and tags show only on page 1.
4. **Share links sit inside the post's sheet**, after the tags, not in the side column: on
   a phone the side column comes last, after the related posts.
5. **The table of contents is in the markup twice**, as a fold (`<details>`) at the top for
   phones and as a sticky note beside the text for wide screens; CSS shows one. A
   no-JS way to have it in both places.
6. **Robots:** `/blog/preview/` is disallowed and also `noindex`; search results are
   `noindex` but not disallowed, so crawlers can read the `noindex` (D19 asked for both
   to be `noindex`).
7. **Category slug `feed` is reserved:** `/blog/section/<s>/feed/` is the section feed, so a
   category with that slug could never be reached. The admin refuses it; a title that would
   make it gets `feed-2`.
8. **Structured data is `BlogPosting`** (a kind of `Article`, as D19 says), plus
   `BreadcrumbList`; the author's `url` appears only while their page exists.
9. **The site's variable font is scoped to `.bl`** (the blog's root), so the other public
   pages render exactly as before.
10. **The navbar's highlight:** `nav_state` marks a row `is-active` when the path is under
    its URL (`/blog/` for every blog page) and adds `aria-current="page"` only on the exact
    page. External links, `#` and the home page never match as a prefix.
11. **The teacher card's link** (D21) needs a public author page: active author with
    `show_profile`, a public post, the card on the teachers page. The card's details open
    for it even without a bio. The landing page shows no link.
12. **Post moments in UTC** (see §2.6).

### 2.6 A bug found: django_jalali on SQLite

On SQLite, `django_jalali`'s `jDateTimeField.get_db_prep_value` stores a datetime as text
with its own offset, and SQLite compares the text. A post loaded (as Tehran time) and saved
again was written as `…+03:30`, which compares 3.5 hours later than the UTC clock, so it left
`public()` for 3.5 hours. PostgreSQL compares real timestamps and was never affected.

Fixed in the blog: `Post.save()` converts the moment fields (`MOMENT_FIELDS`) to UTC, and
every comparison uses `utc_now()` (`neighbours` converts the post's moment too). Test:
`test_saving_a_loaded_post_keeps_its_moment` (fails without the fix).

Not fixed, recorded as a discrepancy (§2.9): every other `jDateTimeField` in the project,
the `auto_now` fields, and the blog's own `prune_images` cutoff on `PostImage.created_at`
compare the same way on SQLite. Production is PostgreSQL, so it matters only for local
SQLite runs.
